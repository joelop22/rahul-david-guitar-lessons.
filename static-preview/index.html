Something else on your mind?
Ask Rahul

I have never played before. Can I join?

Yes. Beginner Guitar starts with holding the instrument, your first chords, simple rhythms and songs. Choose the beginner course when you enquire.

Where do the lessons take place?

Choose live online lessons, in-person lessons in Elamakkara, Kochi, or home tuition. For home tuition, availability and any travel charges are agreed with Rahul before payment.

Can I choose the day and time?

You can share your preferred days and time during admission. Rahul will confirm an available class option with you before payment; a preference is not a confirmed booking.

How do the monthly fees work?

Online lessons are ₹1,800 for four 45-minute lessons. In-person and home tuition are ₹2,000 for four 60-minute lessons, paid in advance. Home tuition may include an agreed travel charge.
